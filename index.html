Our roots

Born in
Santa Cruz.

La Escala was born from a simple belief: that a perfect cup of coffee and a well-made sandwich could change your whole afternoon. We set up on Calle Santo Domingo — in the heart of old Santa Cruz — and haven't looked back.

We built the roastery first. Because if the coffee isn't right, nothing else matters. Our beans come from Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — transformed on site, in small batches, every week.

The food followed the same philosophy. Bread baked at dawn. Granola before the city wakes up. Pastries gone by noon. That's the point.

I
Craft

Every item made by hand. No shortcuts, no compromise on quality.

II
Origin

We know where every bean comes from. Traceability is respect.

III
Community

Locals and visitors, workers and wanderers. Everyone belongs at La Escala.

IV
Place

Rooted in Tenerife. Inspired by the world. This is our island, and we're proud of it.
